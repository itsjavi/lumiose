Some train journeys are a way of getting somewhere. The line over the Bernina Pass is the
destination. It climbs from the Swiss Alps to over two thousand metres without a single rack
section, then drops down towards Italy, past glaciers, frozen lakes and villages that seem to be
holding on to the slope with their fingertips.

## Window seat strategy

- Sit on the right-hand side heading south for the glacier views.
- The panoramic carriages are lovely; the regular regional trains run the same line, open their
  windows and cost much less.
- Bring sunglasses. Snow and sun at altitude are no joke.

## The spiral

Near the end of the line the track has to lose height fast, so it does something wonderful: it
loops around on itself on a stone viaduct, passing under its own arches. From the carriage you can
see the rails you'll be on in a minute, curving away below you.

> Snow muffles everything. Even the train sounded polite.

We arrived in Tirano in sunshine, with palm trees by the station, four hours and one whole climate
after leaving the snow.
